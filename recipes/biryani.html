Chicken Biryani

Description

Chicken biryani is a flavorful rice dish made by cooking fragrant basmati rice with marinated chicken, herbs, and aromatic spices.

Ingredients

  • 500g chicken
  • 2 cups basmati rice
  • 1 onion
  • 2 tomatoes
  • Yogurt
  • Ginger-garlic paste
  • Biryani masala
  • Mint and coriander
  • Salt

Steps

  1. Marinate the chicken with yogurt and spices.
  2. Wash and partially cook the basmati rice.
  3. Cook the marinated chicken with onions and tomatoes.
  4. Layer the rice over the chicken.
  5. Add mint and coriander.
  6. Cover and cook on low heat until the rice is completely cooked.
  7. Serve hot.
