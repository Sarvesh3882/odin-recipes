Butter Chicken

Description

Butter chicken is a rich and creamy Indian dish made with tender chicken cooked in a spiced tomato and butter-based sauce.

Ingredients

  • 500g chicken
  • 2 tablespoons butter
  • 1 cup tomato puree
  • 1/2 cup cream
  • 1 teaspoon garam masala
  • 1 teaspoon chili powder
  • Salt

Steps

  1. Marinate the chicken with spices and yogurt.
  2. Cook the chicken until lightly browned.
  3. Prepare the tomato sauce with butter and spices.
  4. Add the cooked chicken to the sauce.
  5. Add cream and simmer for a few minutes.
  6. Serve hot with rice or naan.
