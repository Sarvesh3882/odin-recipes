Paneer Tikka

Description

Paneer tikka is an Indian appetizer made by marinating pieces of paneer with yogurt and spices before grilling them with vegetables.

Ingredients

  • 250g paneer
  • 1/2 cup yogurt
  • 1 bell pepper
  • 1 onion
  • 1 teaspoon garam masala
  • 1 teaspoon chili powder
  • 1 teaspoon turmeric
  • Salt

Steps

  1. Cut the paneer and vegetables into pieces.
  2. Mix yogurt with the spices.
  3. Add the paneer and vegetables to the marinade.
  4. Let them marinate for at least 30 minutes.
  5. Place the pieces on skewers.
  6. Grill until the paneer is lightly charred.
  7. Serve hot.
